Privacy Policy - NonoHaven

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how NonoHaven (the "Game") handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not collect any information, including personal information. The Game contains no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry. It requires no account and works fully offline.

2. What the Game Stores and Where

The Game is a single-player nonogram (picross) puzzle game. Your game progress, per-puzzle clear records, and settings are stored only in the Game's own local data folder on your PC. They are never sent to any external server. The Game makes no network connections.

3. How to Delete Your Data

Uninstalling the Game removes its stored data from your PC.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

The Game collects no information, so there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: kasayuki05022@gmail.com

6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
